Set up Termix (web terminal) Open Termix and create your account straight away (the first account is the admin), then turn off sign-ups and turn on 2FA. Add this server as a host: address host.docker.internal, port 22, your Linux user, and the private key termix-ssh/id_ed25519 in your app-settings folder (default /srv/appdata) (already trusted by this server). Add your other servers the same way.
